# Third-party notices

Watch Your Speech retains or adapts portions of the following projects. Their
original licenses and copyright notices continue to apply to the relevant
files.

- [LipVoicer](https://github.com/yochaiye/LipVoicer) — the initial
  video-to-speech implementation on which parts of this research code were
  based; MIT license.
- [DiffWave/Sashimi](https://github.com/albertfgu/diffwave-sashimi) — training,
  sampling, and data-loading structure; see the upstream license.
- [Visual Speech Recognition for Multiple Languages](https://github.com/mpc001/Visual_Speech_Recognition_for_Multiple_Languages)
  and its retained ESPnet-derived components — mouth-ROI processing and visual
  speech recognition; Apache-2.0 notices are retained in source files.
- [HiFi-GAN](https://github.com/jik876/hifi-gan) — vocoder implementation and
  configuration; MIT license.
- [VisualVoice](https://github.com/facebookresearch/VisualVoice) and
  [Lipreading using Temporal Convolutional Networks](https://github.com/mpc001/Lipreading_using_Temporal_Convolutional_Networks)
  — visual encoders and data utilities; see the upstream licenses.
- [NVIDIA WaveGlow](https://github.com/NVIDIA/waveglow) — distributed training
  utilities; the BSD-style notice is retained in `distributed_util.py`.
- [pytorch-stft](https://github.com/pseeth/pytorch-stft) and
  [FloWaveNet](https://github.com/ksw0306/FloWaveNet) — STFT and WaveNet
  utilities; see the upstream licenses and source comments.

No third-party model weights or datasets are redistributed in this repository.
